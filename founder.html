Meet the Founder

The Founder

Meet the man behind the mission

Prof. Abanindra Sahu

CS Executive · M.Com (Law)

Abanindra Sahu founded Ambitious Professionals in October 2018 with a clear mission: deliver quality, concept-driven learning in commerce and professional education.

With over 8 years of dedicated teaching experience since founding the institute in 2018, he specialises in Corporate Law and Auditing, mentoring students across multiple academic and professional levels. His classrooms blend academic rigour with real-world insight — turning difficult topics into clear, workable concepts, and helping students build genuine professional confidence, not just exam recall. Teaching Corporate Law to CA Foundation, CMA Foundation & CMA Intermediate students, and Auditing to CMA Intermediate students — with both subjects also open to B.Com (Hons) learners.

A Word From the Founder

Founder's Message

"When I started Ambitious Professionals in 2018, it was with one belief — that every student deserves teaching that goes beyond memorised formulas and actually builds understanding. Over the years, watching students walk in unsure of Corporate Law or Auditing and walk out as qualified professionals has been the most rewarding part of this journey."

"My promise to every student who joins us stays the same: honest guidance, real attention, and a classroom that pushes you toward the result you came here for."

Prof. Abanindra Sahu Founder, Ambitious Professionals

Ambitious Professionals delivers concept-driven CA, CMA and CS coaching in Brahmapur. Since 2018, we've helped students build genuine understanding and achieve real results — not just clear exams.
