Frequently Asked Questions

Questions

Got Questions?

Hover over any question to see the answer.

Do you offer both online and offline classes?
Yes — most of our courses are available in both online live and offline classroom mode. You can choose whichever suits you at the time of enrolment.
Is the free counselling session really free?
Yes, completely — no obligation to enrol afterward. We'll help you plan your course, level, and attempt honestly.
How do I know which paper/faculty to choose?
Our counselling session covers exactly this — or you can call/WhatsApp us directly and we'll guide you paper by paper.
Can I get books along with my classes?
Yes — when booking a class you can choose your book preference (soft copy or hard copy) right in the order form.
Do you offer test series without joining classes?
Yes, our Test Series can be booked independently of classes — chapter-wise or full syllabus.
How do I track my order or contact you after enrolling?
Sign in anytime to see "My Orders" from your profile menu, or reach us directly via Call, WhatsApp, or Email from our Contact page.
What if I need to change my faculty or attempt after ordering?
Just reach out to us via Call or WhatsApp — we're happy to help adjust your order before classes begin.
